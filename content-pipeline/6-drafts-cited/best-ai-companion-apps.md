For adults who want customizable 18+ companionship with persistent chat history, Pleasur.ai is the strongest fit; for non-adult emotional companionship, start with Nomi, Replika, or Wysa.

That answer is intentionally conditional. "Best AI companion app" is not one job.

Some people want a daily emotional check-in. Some want a fictional character chat. Some want an adult companion that can remember a long-running roleplay. Some want a wellness chatbot with stricter guardrails. A useful comparison has to sort those jobs before it ranks the apps.

This best AI companion apps 2026 guide compares Pleasur.ai, Replika, Character.AI, Nomi, Kindroid, Candy AI, Chai, and Wysa. It starts with the criteria that actually decide whether a companion is worth using: memory, privacy and data handling, customization, adult-only boundaries, multimodal features, price and access, and safety controls.

## Criteria Before Ranking

A companion app becomes valuable only when the basics hold up after the first session.

### Memory

Memory is the feature that decides whether an AI companion feels continuous or disposable. Check whether the app only sees the current chat, saves explicit facts, preserves a character backstory, or builds longer-term relationship context.

Nomi says its companions use short, medium, and long-term memory, and its product page frames memory as a core part of enduring relationships: <https://nomi.ai/>. Character.AI announced Story Memory, Facts, and Memory Usage controls in 2026, which matters because older Character.AI comparisons often treated it as mostly session-based: <https://blog.character.ai/memory/>.

For Pleasur.ai, the practical claim is narrower: your chat history and companion setup persist across sessions, which makes it a better fit for an adult roleplay or relationship thread than for generic one-off bot chat. For a deeper memory-only comparison, read the companion cluster page on [AI companion memory](/blog/ai-companion-best-memory).

### Privacy And Data Handling

Memory means data is stored somewhere, so privacy is part of the product, not a footnote.

Before using any AI companion, read the official privacy policy, deletion language, data-sharing language, and app-store data labels. Character.AI's privacy page is here: <https://character.ai/privacy>. Pleasur.ai's privacy policy is here: <https://pleasur.ai/legal/privacy-policy>. Mozilla's privacy reviews of companion and chatbot apps are also a useful outside pressure test, especially for Chai and Youper/Wysa-type products: <https://www.mozillafoundation.org/en/privacynotincluded/chai/>.

Do not treat "private" as a complete claim unless the app tells you what is collected, how long it is kept, whether humans or vendors can access it, and how deletion works.

### Customization

Customization is not just appearance. A useful companion lets you shape persona, backstory, tone, boundaries, and memory.

Kindroid's app-store listing describes shaping a companion through personality, backstory, and key memories. Nomi emphasizes that each companion evolves around your preferences and habits. Pleasur.ai's angle is adult custom companion creation: build the companion, keep the persona consistent, then continue the chat over time.

If you care about fictional characters, Character.AI and Kindroid belong high on your test list. If you care about an adult companion you create and keep using, Pleasur.ai belongs higher.

### Adult-Only Boundaries

Adult support is a category boundary, not a synonym for rule-free chat.

Pleasur.ai is for adults 18+. Candy AI is also adult-positioned. Replika, Nomi, Character.AI, Wysa, and Chai need separate checks because their content boundaries, teen policies, and app-store rules can differ from what a search snippet implies.

Use the [AI companion safety checklist](/blog/ai-companion-safety-checklist) before sharing sensitive details. A credible 18+ product should still prohibit illegal content, real-person abuse, minors, coercion, and unsafe use. No AI companion should be framed as a replacement for therapy, crisis support, legal advice, or a real relationship.

### Multimodal Features

The feature stack changes the experience: text chat, voice, images, calls, avatars, and video are different jobs.

Replika's app listings describe text, voice calls, video, internet access, and image generation. Kindroid describes text chat, AI selfies, and voices. Candy AI markets visual and companion media. Pleasur.ai combines adult chat and AI image generation in one web product, with pricing visible at <https://pleasur.ai/pricing>.

Do not choose on the longest feature list. Choose on the feature you will use weekly, then verify whether it is included, metered, or locked behind credits.

### Price And Access

Price comparisons are only useful when they include the meter.

Some apps use subscription tiers, some use coins or credits, and some combine both. Replika, Nomi, Kindroid, Candy AI, Chai, and Wysa all change pricing or regional availability over time, so treat any static number in a listicle as a starting point, not a promise. Pleasur.ai publishes its current pricing at <https://pleasur.ai/pricing>.

Check four things before paying: monthly price, annual discount, credit rules, and refund/cancellation language.

### Safety Controls

Safety controls are not just filters. They include age rules, reporting, moderation, data deletion, account deletion, crisis language, and clear fictional framing.

Wysa is the cleanest fit when you want structured wellbeing support rather than romantic or adult companionship; its homepage positions it as an evidence-based mental health alternative: <https://www.wysa.com/>. Replika and Character.AI have both added safety and memory features over time, which makes official current pages more reliable than old reviews.

For 18+ companion products, the safety check is different: verify age gating, prohibited content, privacy terms, payment terms, and whether the product avoids pretending the AI is a real person.

## How To Choose An AI Companion App

Use a five-step test instead of trusting a single ranked list.

1. Write the job in one sentence: adult companion, emotional companion, character chat, creative roleplay, or wellness support.
2. Test memory across two sessions: tell the app a specific harmless detail, leave, return later, and see whether it recalls the detail accurately.
3. Read the privacy policy before sharing sensitive details.
4. Check the meter: subscription, credits, messages, images, voice, and cancellation.
5. Confirm boundaries: age rules, adult rules, safety controls, and deletion controls.

That is the best way to avoid the two bad outcomes: a popular app that does not fit your intent, or a permissive-looking app that does not handle privacy and safety well enough for intimate chat.
