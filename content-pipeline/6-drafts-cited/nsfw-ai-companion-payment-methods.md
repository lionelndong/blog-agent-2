## What changed with Eternal AI

Eternal AI built early goodwill with a generous free tier, then changed how paid access works. The company announced it is going "crypto-native," stating that [crypto is now the primary payment method on Eternal AI](https://x.com/EternalAI_/status/2018271071146021012). Independent coverage describes the same model: there are [no monthly subscriptions — paid access is through one-time credit packs](https://scribehow.com/page/Eternal_AI_Review_2026_The_Pay-As-You-Go_Uncensored_Companion_That_Breaks_the_Subscription_Mold__vGEm7M-4Sl-HQbFwFT7q6g), and on the website those packs are bought with cryptocurrency.

There's a real-world echo of this in community threads, where users report that the site now expects crypto to upgrade. It's worth being precise, though: Eternal AI's iOS and Android apps still accept normal card payments plus Apple Pay and Google Pay. So it isn't that you *can't* pay with a card anywhere — it's that the **web checkout is crypto-first**, which is enough friction to send mainstream users looking elsewhere. None of this is a knock on the product; crypto-first billing is a deliberate privacy choice. It just isn't how most people pay.
