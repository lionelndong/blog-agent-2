## Why people are leaving JanitorAI in 2026

JanitorAI's problem isn't the characters. It's that the platform asks you to be your own backend, and that breaks in three predictable ways.

**Downtime and stalls.** JanitorAI leans on heavy traffic and third-party model routes, so outages and "the bot isn't responding" stretches are common enough that "is JanitorAI down" is one of the most-searched things about the product. When the route you rely on goes dark, your chat goes with it.

**Proxy and API-key setup.** To run a strong uncensored model on JanitorAI, you connect it yourself. The platform's own [beginner's guide to using a proxy](https://help.janitorai.com/en/article/the-absolute-beginners-guide-to-using-a-proxy-with-janitor-part-one-19to7y9/) walks through pasting in a proxy URL, generating an API key, and matching a model name — and warns that mixing credentials from different providers is the number-one cause of errors. People also search "how to set up DeepSeek on JanitorAI" thousands of times a month, which tells you how much friction that step adds. If "create an account on a model provider, generate a key, and paste three fields correctly" sounds like a chore, that's because it is.

**NSFW quality is only as good as your proxy.** JanitorAI's built-in model handles adult roleplay (18+), but the memorable replies usually come from a model you bolt on. Pick a weak free route and the writing is thin; pick a strong paid one and your costs float with usage. Either way the experience isn't consistent.

The alternatives below fix this by hosting their own model. You sign in, pick a character, and start — no proxy, no key, no model name to match. That single difference is why most people switch.

## The 8 best JanitorAI alternatives in 2026

### 1. SpicyChat — the best free uncensored pick

If your only must-have is "free and uncensored without the proxy circus," start here. SpicyChat hosts its own adult-tuned models, so you sign in and roleplay — no key, no proxy. Adult roleplay (18+) works out of the box, and there's a large community character library plus your own character creator.

The free tier is real, not a teaser: roughly [100 messages a day](https://aicompanionguides.com/blog/spicychat-ai-review-2026/), with ads and a queue at busy times as the trade-off. Heavy chatters can jump to Premium at $14.95/mo for unlimited messages and no line.

Its weak spot is memory — shorter than a dedicated companion app, so multi-day storylines drift and characters forget details you established last week. For the JanitorAI refugee who wants the same anything-goes feel and never wants to see a settings screen, that's a fair trade.

### 2. CrushOn.ai — the biggest uncensored character library

CrushOn is the closest thing to "JanitorAI, but hosted for you." It's built around uncensored adult roleplay (18+) with one of the deepest community character catalogs in the space, so the "let me browse thousands of characters" itch JanitorAI scratched is covered here too — without the wiring.

Its pricing is the friendliest of the hosted NSFW apps. There's a free tier on the basic models, then [Standard at $4.90/mo and Premium at $7.90/mo](https://crushon.ai/pricing) unlock the better models and higher limits, model included. The honest catch: the strongest models live behind those paid tiers, and the free models are noticeably weaker than what you'd get on a well-configured JanitorAI proxy. Pay the $4.90 and that gap closes fast.

### 3. Nomi AI — the best memory and emotional depth

Most apps on this list forget your last scene. Nomi doesn't. It's a companion-first platform built around long-term memory, and it [describes itself as unfiltered and uncensored](https://nomi.ai/), so adult conversation (18+) sits alongside genuine companionship rather than replacing it. Voice messages and hands-free calls are built in, which makes Nomi feel less like a chatbot you prompt and more like someone you keep up with.

You can start free, but treat that as a trial: daily message caps, one companion, no voice. The real product is the subscription at about [$15.99/mo, or roughly $8.33/mo paid annually](https://autogpt.net/nomi-ai-pricing/), which opens up unlimited chat, multiple companions, voice, and images. It's the priciest monthly rate here, and it leans companionship over raw kink — so if you want a partner who remembers what you told them, that premium is the point. If you just want quick scenes, it's overkill.

### 4. Character.AI — the polished mainstream option

Two honest sentences first: Character.AI is the most refined chat experience on this page, and it is **not** an uncensored alternative. Its filter blocks explicit roleplay — the exact wall that pushed people to JanitorAI in the first place.

So why is it here? Because a real slice of JanitorAI users care more about clever, deeply in-character conversation than about adult content, and nobody does that better than Character.AI. The library is enormous, the apps are smooth, and it's free with queues. [c.ai+ at $9.99/mo](https://character.ai/subscription/plus/pricing) buys faster replies, priority access, and better memory. Come here for polish and personality; do not come here expecting NSFW, because that filter isn't loosening.

### 5. Replika — the companion you keep for years

Replika isn't a roleplay-character app at all — it's a single AI companion that grows with you, holding your routines, your history, and the things you're working through. For long-term emotional connection, it's the most mature product in the category.

The core companion is free, and [Replika Pro at $19.99/mo or $69.99/yr](https://help.replika.com/hc/en-us/articles/39551043419149-Choosing-a-Subscription) adds relationship modes and voice calls. Where it falls short as a JanitorAI swap is the obvious place: adult content is deliberately limited, leaning romance and intimacy over explicit roleplay. If NSFW was the whole reason you used JanitorAI, this isn't your app. If you wanted something steadier than a character you reset every night, it might be the best one here.

### 6. Chai — the mobile-first quick-chat app

Chai lives on your phone. Open the app, swipe through community bots, start a scene — it's the fastest "I'm bored, entertain me" experience on the list, with NSFW allowed inside its guidelines.

Go in knowing two things. The free tier has tightened to roughly [50–100 messages a day](https://www.chai-ai.com/pricing), and Chai [rolled out a regional subscription paywall in 2026](https://www.roborhythms.com/chai-ai-subscription-paywall-2026/) that hit some users with hard limits overnight. Premium at $13.99/mo restores unlimited chat and better models. It's mobile-only and the free experience is shrinking, so think of Chai as a casual phone habit rather than your main platform.

### 7. Talkie AI — the best for voice-led roleplay

Talkie treats voice and personality as the main event, not an add-on. Characters speak in distinct voices, react with more performed, story-driven energy than the text-only apps, and the whole thing is built to be heard as much as read. NSFW is tolerated within its rules, it's free to start with the usual daily limits, and paid tiers cover heavier use.

The flip side of that polish is that Talkie skews playful and gamified — daily missions, collectible characters, a lighter touch overall. Hardcore roleplayers chasing the unfiltered depth of a JanitorAI setup will find it tame. But if you've always wanted to actually hear your character instead of reading walls of text, nothing else here does voice this naturally.

### 8. SillyTavern — for power users who actually liked the flexibility

Here's the honest one. If what you loved about JanitorAI was the *control* — choosing your own model, tuning prompts, importing character cards — don't downgrade to a closed app. Use SillyTavern.

It's a free, open-source front-end that [doesn't run the AI itself; you bring your own model or API](https://weavai.app/blog/en/2026/04/18/sillytavern-review-2026-ultimate-ai-roleplay-guide/), and in return it hands you the richest roleplay toolkit anywhere: lorebooks, fine-grained sampler control, world info, and a character-card system that reads thousands of community PNGs. The catch is the one you came here to escape — it needs a model, so you're right back to connecting an API key or proxy and paying for whatever you plug in. SillyTavern is the only pick on this list that doesn't solve the setup problem. It embraces it, in exchange for control nothing else offers. For technical users, that's not a bug; it's the whole appeal.

## Who should actually stay on JanitorAI

Most listicles won't say this, so here it is: not everyone should switch. If you've already got a working proxy, you're happy with your model's writing, and the downtime doesn't bother you, JanitorAI still gives you free characters and total model choice that no single hosted app fully matches. The people who should move are the ones spending more time fixing "Network Error" than roleplaying, or who never wanted to manage a model in the first place. If that's not you, the best alternative might be the setup you already have.

## Frequently asked questions

**What is the best free JanitorAI alternative?**
SpicyChat is the best genuinely-free uncensored option — it hosts its own adult model, so you get roleplay (18+) with no proxy or API key, on a free tier of about 100 messages a day. CrushOn.ai is the runner-up, with a free tier and paid plans from $4.90/mo.

**What is the best JanitorAI alternative for NSFW?**
For hosted, no-setup adult roleplay, SpicyChat and CrushOn.ai lead. If you want adult chat plus strong memory and voice, Nomi AI is the better fit. All three handle 18+ content without you wiring up a model.

**Why is JanitorAI down or so slow?**
JanitorAI depends on heavy traffic and third-party model routes, so outages and stalls are common, and the experience degrades when a proxy or model route fails. Apps that host their own model — like the ones above — avoid that failure mode.

**Do JanitorAI alternatives need a proxy or API key?**
Most don't. SpicyChat, CrushOn.ai, Nomi, Character.AI, Replika, Chai, Talkie, and Pleasur.ai all run their own models, so you just sign in and chat. The only exception here is SillyTavern, which is a front-end and still needs you to connect a model.

**Is JanitorAI safe to use?**
JanitorAI is a real platform, but because you often route chats through third-party proxies, your messages can pass through services you don't control. Treat anything you send as potentially logged, avoid sharing identifying details, and prefer apps with a clear, published privacy policy.
