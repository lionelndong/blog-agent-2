Start with your product job. Do you want a public character library, or one recurring companion? Do you care most about content boundaries, memory, voice, images, mobile convenience, or model control? Do you need a hosted app, or are you comfortable managing a local setup?

Then check the policies. Before you share personal details, inspect age rules, fictional disclosure, sensitive-data warnings, chat data use, deletion and retention language, moderation, and entertainment-only framing. If you cannot find those answers, treat that as part of your decision.

Pleasur.ai's [AI companion safety checklist](/blog/ai-companion-safety-checklist) is the right support page for that due diligence. Use it as your checklist, not as a promise that any one platform is safe, private, or right for you.

Be especially careful with claims like "free," "unlimited," "no signup," and "no filter." These change often and can hide important tradeoffs. A free tier may have quotas. A no-filter claim may still have moderation. A privacy claim may not match how chat content is used for operations, analytics, or model improvement.

This guide uses public positioning and sourceable capabilities. It does not rank apps by unsupported superiority claims, private safety assumptions, or volatile pricing.

## Safety And Privacy Checklist Before Switching

Before using any Character.AI alternative, check the age rules, data-use language, moderation policy, deletion terms, and emotional-boundary framing.

[VISUAL:type=image;sub=diagram;prompt=Safety and privacy checklist for AI companion alternatives. Items: age rules, fictional disclosure, sensitive-data warning, chat-content use, deletion and retention, moderation, entertainment versus therapy framing. Include where to verify: terms, privacy policy, safety center, help center. Clean labels, no explicit content;style=flat-vector;safety=sfw]

Ask these questions before you share sensitive details:

- **Age rules.** Is the product adult-only, teen-restricted, or general audience?
- **Fictional disclosure.** Does it clearly state AI characters are fictional?
- **Sensitive-data warning.** Does it tell users not to share passwords, addresses, financial details, government IDs, or other sensitive information?
- **Chat-content use.** Can the company use chat content for personalization, moderation, service improvement, analytics, or model training?
- **Deletion and retention.** Can you delete data, and what might still be retained?
- **Content moderation.** What is prohibited, and what happens if content is flagged?
- **Emotional boundaries.** Does the product frame itself as entertainment or companionship, or does it drift into therapy-like claims?

Pleasur.ai's Terms are a useful example of what to inspect. They include adult-only language, entertainment-only framing, fictional companion disclosure, sensitive-information warnings, moderation language, and content-use language ([Pleasur.ai Terms](https://pleasur.ai/legal/terms-of-service)). That does not prove any platform is safe or private. It shows you the kinds of policy claims to read before creating an account.

Category scrutiny is increasing. The FTC opened a 2025 inquiry into consumer-facing AI companion chatbots focused on how companies measure, test, and monitor potential negative impacts on children and teens ([FTC](https://www.ftc.gov/news-events/news/press-releases/2025/09/ftc-launches-inquiry-ai-chatbots-acting-companions)). Common Sense Media also published a 2025 social AI companion assessment recommending that minors under 18 not use social AI companions ([Common Sense Media](https://www.commonsensemedia.org/press-releases/ai-companions-decoded-common-sense-media-recommends-ai-companion-safety-standards)).

This is not legal advice. It is basic due diligence for a category where private-feeling conversations can become personal very quickly.

### What is the best free Character.AI alternative?

Free access, quotas, and feature limits change often. Verify current pricing and limits on official product pages before choosing. A product can be free to start and still limit your messages, images, models, memory, voice, or other features.

### Is Character.AI still worth using?

Yes, when mainstream character discovery, community-created characters, and low setup friction matter more than adult-only positioning, roleplay flexibility, local control, or one recurring companion. If Character.AI still solves the job, switching may create more friction than it removes.

### Are Character.AI alternatives safe or private?

They vary. Read each product's age rules, privacy policy, terms, moderation policy, deletion language, and sensitive-data warnings. Avoid sharing private information until you understand how the service can use, moderate, store, or retain chat content.
