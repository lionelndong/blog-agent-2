- Background paper white `#FBFBFA` with very subtle grain
- Primary charcoal type/lines `#1A1A1A`
- Neutral mid-grey `#8A8F98` (secondary lines, dotted connectors)

**Typography:** clean geometric sans-serif (like Inter or Söhne), medium weight for node labels,
small tracked uppercase for the title strip. In-image text kept short per element to avoid mangling.
