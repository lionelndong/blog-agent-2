## VISUAL 1 — concept-illustration: Replika change timeline (intro section)

**Placeholder:** `[VISUAL:type=image;sub=concept-illustration; ... 2017 Launch / Feb 2023 ERP removed / May 2023 Legacy reversal / 2025 New CEO, 40M users ...]`
**Archetype:** Editorial diagram (concept made spatial — a horizontal milestone timeline).
**File:** `image-1-what-happened-to-replika-users.png`

### 9-part prompt

1. **Subject declaration** — Horizontal editorial diagram for a premium technology-and-relationships blog article about how a popular AI-companion app changed over time. A clean minimalist horizontal milestone timeline, modern editorial-infographic aesthetic, calm-but-serious explanatory mood.

2. **Canvas** — Full-width horizontal 3:2 in-article composition. Solid off-white background (#FAF8F5) with a very subtle paper grain; generous whitespace, nothing cluttered.

3. **Composition map** — A single thin horizontal baseline runs left-to-right across the vertical middle of the frame, spanning ~88% width, centered. Four evenly spaced milestone dots sit on the line. Each milestone has a short vertical tick connecting the dot to a small stacked caption (year on top in bold, one short phrase below in regular weight), captions alternating slightly above/below the line for breathing room. A faint thin arrowhead terminates the line at the far right to signal forward time.

4. **Per-element specification** —
   - Baseline: 2px hairline stroke in slate-grey (#5B6470), perfectly straight, soft rounded ends.
   - Dot 1 (far left, ~10% from left): solid filled circle ~14px in muted teal (#3E7C84), micro-detail: a faint concentric halo ring. Caption "2017" bold, "Launch" below.
   - Dot 2 (~37%): solid filled circle in muted amber-clay (#C8804A), micro-detail: a tiny hairline crack glyph beside it suggesting disruption. Caption "FEB 2023" bold, "Features removed" below.
   - Dot 3 (~63%): solid filled circle in soft sage (#7E9B6E), micro-detail: a small partial-arc "return" curve. Caption "MAY 2023" bold, "Partial reversal" below.
   - Dot 4 (far right, ~88%): solid filled circle in deep indigo (#3D4A7A), micro-detail: a small upward spark/up-tick. Caption "2025" bold, "New CEO · 40M users" below.
   - All dots same diameter, evenly weighted; ticks are 1px slate-grey.

5. **Character & emotion** — Objects, not people. The emotional arc reads through color temperature: teal optimism at launch → clay-orange rupture in 2023 → green relief in the partial reversal → confident indigo at the present. Restrained, documentary, trustworthy — not playful, not alarmist.

6. **Style triangulation** — Swiss editorial infographic meets premium fintech explainer graphic — flat vector, precise alignment, lots of air. NOT 3D, NOT skeuomorphic, NOT hand-drawn doodle, NOT neon, NOT clip-art, NO photo textures, NO gradients on the dots.

7. **Lighting & render spec** — Flat even illumination, no cast shadows on the dots, no bevels; pure flat-vector rendering with crisp anti-aliased edges. Optional single very soft drop-shadow under the baseline only, barely perceptible.

8. **Palette block** — background off-white #FAF8F5; baseline/ticks slate-grey #5B6470; dot 1 teal #3E7C84; dot 2 amber-clay #C8804A; dot 3 sage #7E9B6E; dot 4 indigo #3D4A7A; caption text charcoal #2A2D34.

9. **Typography block** — clean geometric sans-serif (like Inter or Söhne), years in bold uppercase small-caps, phrases in regular weight sentence case, tight tracking. Exact strings ONLY: "2017", "Launch", "FEB 2023", "Features removed", "MAY 2023", "Partial reversal", "2025", "New CEO · 40M users". No other text anywhere.

**Mood line + quality anchor:** calm, documentary, precise, trustworthy editorial infographic — Awwwards / Behance site-of-the-day quality, 4k, crisp vector.

## VISUAL 2 — concept-illustration: Feature-stability checklist (how-to-pick section)

**Placeholder:** `[VISUAL:type=image;sub=concept-illustration; ... Vertical editorial checklist "Feature-stability check", five rows ...]`
**Archetype:** Editorial diagram (vertical checklist card).
**File:** `image-2-what-happened-to-replika-users.png`

### 9-part prompt

1. **Subject declaration** — Vertical editorial illustration for a premium technology blog: a clean five-item "feature-stability checklist" card that helps a reader vet an app before committing. Minimalist editorial-infographic aesthetic, practical and reassuring mood.

2. **Canvas** — Portrait 4:5 in-article composition. Soft warm off-white background (#FAF8F5) with subtle paper grain; a single centered rounded-rectangle card floating with generous margin.

3. **Composition map** — One rounded-corner card spanning ~70% width, centered, occupying ~85% height. Card header strip at top holds a short title. Below it, five evenly spaced horizontal checklist rows stack vertically; each row = a square checkbox glyph on the left (~10% width) + a short label to its right. A thin hairline divider separates each row.

4. **Per-element specification** —
   - Card: matte off-white fill (#FFFFFF) with a 1.5px slate-grey border (#5B6470), softly rounded 24px corners, micro-detail: a barely-there top-edge highlight.
   - Header strip: muted indigo band (#3D4A7A) with the title reversed out in off-white.
   - Five checkboxes: rounded-square outlines in slate-grey; each contains a clean teal check-mark (#3E7C84), micro-detail: check strokes have rounded caps and a slight hand-confident weight; all five identical and aligned.
   - Row labels: charcoal text, single line each, left-aligned, consistent baseline.
   - Hairline dividers: 1px #E3DFD8 between rows.

5. **Character & emotion** — No people. Tone is calm competence — "do this and you won't get burned again." Orderly, even spacing signals reliability and control.

6. **Style triangulation** — Clean SaaS-onboarding checklist UI meets print editorial infographic — flat vector, generous whitespace. NOT 3D, NOT glossy, NOT neon, NOT hand-drawn, NO photo textures, NO drop-shadow stacks, NO clip-art checkmarks.

7. **Lighting & render spec** — Flat even light, one very soft single drop-shadow under the whole card to lift it off the background; otherwise no bevels or inner shadows. Crisp anti-aliased flat-vector rendering.

8. **Palette block** — background #FAF8F5; card #FFFFFF; border/checkbox outline slate-grey #5B6470; header band indigo #3D4A7A; check-marks teal #3E7C84; labels charcoal #2A2D34; dividers #E3DFD8.

9. **Typography block** — clean geometric sans-serif (Inter/Söhne). Title bold; labels medium weight, sentence case, short. Exact strings ONLY — Title: "Feature-stability check". Rows: "Removed features before?", "Adult chat for all paid users?", "Persistent memory?", "Changes announced first?", "Regulator actions?". No other text.

**Mood line + quality anchor:** practical, reassuring, orderly, premium editorial checklist — Awwwards / Behance quality, 4k, crisp flat vector.

## Art-director outcome (final, post-generation)

**Model:** black-forest-labs/flux-schnell ($0.003/img). Both concept-illustrations 1200×1200, brand-neutral, NO people, NO real logos, 18+ neutral — compliant.

- **VISUAL 1 (timeline):** original placeholder prompt → garbled captions, 3 dots (FAIL). Root cause: anatomy part 9 (typography) — flux-schnell cannot render multi-label in-image text. 2 retries; landed a fully textless minimal timeline (grey baseline + arrowhead + evenly-spaced colored dots). Captions are intentionally omitted (downstream/editor adds them). ACCEPTABLE.
- **VISUAL 2 (checklist):** original placeholder prompt → garbled title + 5 nonsense rows, 6 boxes (FAIL). Same root cause. 2 retries; landed a clean premium checklist card (indigo/teal header band, 4 checkbox rows, 2 checked, soft drop shadow), fully textless. ACCEPTABLE.

**Lesson:** for text-heavy editorial diagrams, flux-schnell must be prompted textless — never rely on it to render labels. Row/caption text belongs in markdown or a downstream typesetting pass, not baked into the flux render.
