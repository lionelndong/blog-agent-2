## Deep web research findings (Perplexity / sonar-reasoning-pro)

- **Metering is universal but unadmitted.** "No major AI companion platform — including Pleasur.ai — offers truly unlimited media generation; all implement some form of usage metering even at premium price points, creating consumer confusion about what 'premium' actually unlocks." This is the strongest information-gain hook: the whole category meters, but only platforms that *publish* allowances + per-action costs let you calculate it.
- **Annual-billing distortion.** "The discourse largely ignores the significant price differentiation between monthly and annual billing… few comparative analyses properly account for billing structure," producing misleading apples-to-oranges comparisons. Our Model + annual columns fix this directly.
- **Coin depletion is the silent surprise.** "Review sites mention metering but rarely emphasize how quickly coin allowances deplete during regular use, leaving users surprised by limitations despite paying for 'premium' service." → motivates the "how fast do coins burn" worked example.
- Stale-figure caution captured above ($19 tier; video pricing) — both flagged, neither load-bearing.
