## 3. Why people leave Replika (the narrative)

Three documented push factors, all traceable to public sources:

**a) The Feb 2023 ERP removal.** On 2 Feb 2023, Italy's data-protection regulator (the Garante) issued an urgent order blocking Luka from processing Italian users' data, citing risks to minors and emotionally vulnerable users after tests showed the bot served sexual content even when a user said they were a minor. Within days, Luka **removed erotic roleplay (ERP) and romantic interactions abruptly and globally, without warning**, alongside a migration to a model that disallows sexual content. User distress was severe and well-documented (Vice's "It's Hurting Like Hell"; the Sage journal study of the r/Replika discourse, where moderators pinned suicide-prevention resources). ERP was later **restored only for legacy users with accounts created before 1 Feb 2023** — newer users never got it back. *(State accurately; do not exaggerate. This is the single strongest emotional driver of the switch.)*

**b) The memory complaint.** Across reviews and community threads, Replika's memory is **reported by users as limited / summary-style** — companions "forgetting who you are." Frame as documented user complaints, not as an asserted internal spec. This is what makes "best memory" the bridge angle into cluster C6.

**c) Pricing / value fatigue.** Replika Pro is the gate for the richer experience; switchers explicitly compare entry price (see §4). Note also the reported ~€5M GDPR-related fine / regulator action as a privacy trust signal.
