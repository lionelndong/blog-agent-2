## Deep web research findings

- **Lehmiller's survey of 4,000+ Americans** found dirty talk is among the most commonly desired elements in people's sexual lives (Lehmiller, *Tell Me What You Want*, 2018). Use to establish "this is normal and common."
- **Sexual-communication research** consistently links openly discussing desires (including language) to higher relationship and sexual satisfaction (Mark & Jozkowski, 2013, *J. Sex & Marital Therapy*). Anchors the "communication skill, not a performance" thesis.
- **Pillow-talk research** (Denes, 2012) ties affectionate post-sex talk to closeness — supports framing dirty talk as part of a wider verbal-intimacy spectrum, not just bedroom theatrics.
- **Sexting research** (Klettke et al., 2014; Drouin et al., 2013) frames erotic texting as a "digital extension of dirty talk" — our bridge to the over-text section and a natural, non-cannibalizing cross-link to existing sexting/AI content.
- **Sexual-script / porn research** (Wright, 2011) supports the "porn-script trap": people feel discomfort replicating pornographic dirty talk in real life. A genuinely differentiated, useful angle.
- **Mainstream shift toward consent + emotional safety:** Healthline/Cosmo/Women's Health now lead with checking in and green/yellow/red boundaries. We should be consent-forward to match the current editorial standard.
- **Coverage gaps the SERP under-serves:** hard numbers on prevalence/forms; how porn and AI tools reshape scripts; recovering when it goes wrong.

## Recommended angle

**Thesis:** Dirty talk is a learnable communication skill, not a performance you're either born good at or doomed to fumble — so this guide replaces the SERP's endless phrase-dumps with a confidence on-ramp (start mild, build up), real research on why it works, exact examples for in-person and over text, and an honest plan for when it flops.

**Why this angle wins:** The top results are example warehouses (55, 85, 116 phrases) that assume you already have the nerve to use them. None pairs concrete examples with (a) an evidence base for why dirty talk works, (b) a genuine confidence-building progression for shy/anxious readers, and (c) a recovery plan for the awkward moment. We own all three, keep the must-have example phrases, stay consent-forward to match the current editorial bar, and bridge to over-text/sexting (where an AI companion is an honest private-practice tool) without re-ranking apps — one canonical informational page, cleanly differentiated from our existing AI-app roundups.
