Janitor AI has carved a niche as one of the personalized AI chatbots.  It is known for its excellence in allowing users to create and interact with virtual characters.

However, there are deficiencies such as limited customization, inadequate responses, the probability of being biased, content policies, and limited knowledge.

This prompts users to seek other options. In this article, we list the top 10 alternatives to Janitor AI, highlighting their key features, pros, and cons.

## **Best Janitor AI Alternatives**

### **1\.** [**Botify AI**](https://botify.ai/)

![Botify AI, a Janitor AI alternative](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%200%200'%3E%3C/svg%3E)

Botify AI allows users to create their own type of custom bots and virtual assistants. It could be an anime or AI character. Unlike most bots, Botify permits users to juggle between SFW and NSFW modes.

#### **Key Features**

- Multiple languages available.
- NSFW feature available (it can be disabled at any time.
- Create a conversational system without the use of code.
- Enhance customer interactions with the integration of CRMs and APIs.

#### **Pricing**

- The basic plan is free.
- Premium plan costs $9.99 monthly.

#### **Pros**

- Flexible character customization.
- The basic plan is free.
- Good long-term memory, which allows users to build on complex storylines.
- Enables more interactions by focusing on user intent and providing relevant answers based on the individual’s needs.

#### **Cons**

- The free plan has limits.
- Limitations in Natural Language Programming (NLP) customization.
- Delayed response under traffic.
- Group chat options are limited.

#### **Pricing**

#### **Cons**

- Occasional short-term memory
- Inability to create private characters, as all are either public or unlisted.
