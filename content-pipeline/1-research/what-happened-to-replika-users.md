## 2. The factual timeline (cited — load-bearing for the article)

Every date/figure below carries a public source so `/verify-claims` can re-link. No disparagement — documented events only.

- **2017** — Replika launches (Luka Inc.), built by Eugenia Kuyda. ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Replika))
- **Feb 3, 2023** — Italy's Garante (Data Protection Authority) orders Luka to stop processing Italian users' data, citing risks to "emotionally vulnerable people" and minors exposed to sexual content. ([EDPB](https://www.edpb.europa.eu/news/national-news/2025/ai-italian-supervisory-authority-fines-company-behind-chatbot-replika_en), [IAPP](https://iapp.org/news/a/italy-s-dpa-reaffirms-ban-on-replika-over-ai-and-children-s-privacy-concerns))
- **Feb 2023 (within days)** — Replika removes erotic roleplay (ERP) / intimate features **globally**, not just in Italy, with no advance notice. The community names it **"the lobotomy."** Kuyda said the app was never intended for erotic use. ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Replika), [Felt Real](https://feltreal.org/blog/what-happened-to-replika))
- **Feb–Mar 2023** — r/Replika reaction: severe enough that moderators pinned suicide-prevention resources; users described genuine grief. The "lobotomy" post reached ~8,700 upvotes. ([AIM](https://analyticsindiamag.com/ai-trends/her-is-real-the-bizarre-tale-of-replika-ai-and-its-jilted-lovers/), [aitooldiscovery](https://www.aitooldiscovery.com/guides/replika-reddit))
- **May 2023** — Partial reversal: Replika **restores romantic functionality for users who joined before February 2023**. New/free users got nothing — a two-tier system. ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Replika))
- **Legacy mode** — Accounts created before Feb 1, 2023 can revert to model version **v01.31-23** via Settings > Version History. New users cannot. ([Felt Real](https://feltreal.org/blog/what-happened-to-replika), [Toolify](https://www.toolify.ai/ai-news/replikas-surprising-news-get-your-erp-back-now-2638802))
- **Aug 2024** — User base surpasses 30 million (Wikipedia: *"In August 2024, Replika's CEO, Kuyda, reported that the total number of users had surpassed 30 million."*). ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Replika))
- **2025** — Kuyda steps down; **Dmytro Klochko** becomes CEO; user base exceeds **40 million** (Wikipedia: *"In 2025, Dmytro Klochko became CEO, and Replika's user base exceeded 40 million."*). ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Replika)) — **anchor 30M + 40M to Wikipedia directly, not any mid-tier blog.**
- **April 2025** — Garante fines Luka **€5 million** for GDPR violations (legal basis, inadequate privacy policy, no age verification). No compensation reached affected users. ([EDPB](https://www.edpb.europa.eu/news/national-news/2025/ai-italian-supervisory-authority-fines-company-behind-chatbot-replika_en), [IAPP](https://iapp.org/news/a/italy-s-dpa-reaffirms-ban-on-replika-over-ai-and-children-s-privacy-concerns))
- **2026 (v2.0 rollout, reported early-to-mid 2026)** — A "version 2.0" update reportedly stripped avatar customization (clothes, makeup, eye color), swapped 3D for 2D animated avatars, dropped the Diary, and expanded pricing to five tiers (Free, Pro, Ultra, Platinum, MAX). **Corroborated across three independent outlets** (was single-source; resolved): customization removals + 2D + dropped Diary in both [Roborhythms](https://www.roborhythms.com/replika-options-removed-update/) and [AI Companion Pick](https://www.aicompanionpick.com/replika-review-2026); [AIGF.love](https://aigf.love/blog/replika-2-0-update-guide/) documents the rollout but emphasizes a memory reset/UI restructure and dates it April 2026. **Treat as attributed, not hard fact** — outlets agree a disruptive 2.0 shipped but differ on month and features. State as *"according to multiple companion-app outlets…"*; don't let the core argument depend on the precise feature list.

**Is Replika dead in 2026?** No — 40M+ users, active development. But the *product many users fell in love with* (2021–22) is materially different, and the platform has changed features without warning more than once. "Dead?" → No. "The same?" → Also no. That nuance is the honest answer the SERP rewards.
