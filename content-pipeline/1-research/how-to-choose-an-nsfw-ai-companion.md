## Deep web research findings

Deep research (sonar-reasoning-pro) was hardened with live source resolution (WebSearch/WebFetch, 2026-06-13) so every load-bearing number below is hyperlinkable. Key signals:

- **Surprise 1 — the "advertised price" is a bait number; real cost runs 3–4× higher.** On Candy AI the headline **$5.99/mo** is the *annual* rate; month-to-month is **$12.99/mo** (2.2× markup), quarterly **$9.99/mo**. Every paid tier includes only **100 monthly tokens** for images/voice (2–5 per image, 1 per voice min), steering heavy users into add-on packs ($9.99/100 up to $299.99/3,750); reviewers say budget **$15–25/mo extra** in tokens ([Candy AI pricing, OhGirlfriend, 2026](https://ohgirlfriend.com/pricing/candy-ai-pricing/)). The pricing page understates real cost 3–4×. *Guide move: read the annual-vs-monthly gap and the metered-token line, not the hero price.*
- **Pricing also conflicts across the category — map tiers, don't trust one figure.** Replika gates ERP behind **Pro $19.99/mo** ($69.99/yr) + 18+ verification ([AI Companion Guides, 2026](https://aicompanionguides.com/blog/replika-review/)); Digital Girlfriend AI is a flat **$19.99/mo** ([Scribe, 2026](https://scribehow.com/page/Digital_Girlfriend_AI_Review_Pricing_Value_and_Reality_in_2026__h79A2f5_QxmKsDlkUMcW1g)); Privee AI from $12/mo, CrushOn.AI from $5.99/mo ([AI Journal, 2026](https://aijourn.com/best-nsfw-ai-chat-sites-top-11-nsfw-adult-ai-chatbots-of-2025/)). Same promise, prices spanning 2–4×.
- **Context (not a surprise): the category is going multi-modal** (text → image → voice/video; metered voice minutes + on-demand images). Feeds the capability criteria, but this has been the pitch since ~2020 — *not* an insight.
- **Surprise 2 — no independent privacy scrutiny, and the documented defaults cut against the user.** Reviews reassure ("safe," "anonymous") but show **no breach history, no retention detail, no audits.** Where retention *is* documented it is adverse: Digital Girlfriend AI **retains chat logs for "training/improvement" unless you opt out**, with a **7-day "undelete window"** on account deletion ([Scribe, 2026](https://scribehow.com/page/Digital_Girlfriend_AI_Review_Pricing_Value_and_Reality_in_2026__h79A2f5_QxmKsDlkUMcW1g)). Industry pattern: free/standard tiers train on conversations by default with a buried opt-out, and "history off" still means ~30-day retention ([retention patterns, 2026](https://lumichats.com/blog/ai-privacy-2026-what-chatgpt-claude-gemini-grok-do-with-your-data)) — the most explicit data a user generates is the hardest to keep out of training. Strongest information-gain lane; aligns with the content-policy guardrail. (Pricing-maze and no-privacy-scrutiny are the two surprises that hold; the old "multi-modal/emotional" surprise is demoted to context above.)
