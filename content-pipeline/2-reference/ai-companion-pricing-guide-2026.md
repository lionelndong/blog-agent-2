## Voice / framing notes

- The brand's pricing voice is **honest-broker**: name the trick ("the hero price is a bait number"), show the math, place our own product inside the comparison rather than above it.
- Differentiator is **transparency**, never "no fees / unlimited / flat rate" — published allowances + published per-action costs you can calculate upfront, vs opaque token burn.
- Descriptive anchors only (match target H1); plain 8th–9th grade English; every competitor figure source-attributed and dated.
