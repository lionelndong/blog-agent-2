## Features to showcase (primary)

### Persistent memory layer — within AI Companion Creator (status: live — re-verified 2026-06-28)
- **What it is:** The companion saves key facts, preferences, and relationship history between sessions, so chats resume with continuity instead of from a blank slate. Frame it as a persistent memory *layer/architecture* sitting on top of the chat, vs. a fixed context window that drops earlier exchanges once a conversation gets long.
- **Use-case for THIS article:** The thesis anchor. Memory is the root failure — an app that forgets you can't hold a consistent character, can't avoid repetition, and can't justify its paywall. This is the load-bearing product claim.
- **Maps to body sections:**
  - **H2 #1 Poor memory / forgetting** (the lead failure, ties to the H1) — fixed-context-window explanation → persistent memory layer as the fix.
  - **H2 #3 Fake / inconsistent characters (personality drift)** — persistence is what lets a persona stay consistent across sessions.
- **How to demonstrate:** First-hand walkthrough + screenshot showing the companion recalling a detail from an earlier session unprompted ("remembered a fact you mentioned days ago"). This is the information-gain no SERP page has. Do NOT use the "82% memory" stat first-person — only as an attributed MariaVibe citation with URL, if at all. Do NOT counter any competitor "Nx recall" claim with a fabricated number.

### Real-time two-way Phone Call (in-chat) + Voice Replies / voice notes (status: live — re-verified 2026-06-28)
- **What it is:** Two in-chat capabilities of the AI Companion Creator (no standalone product, no dedicated `/voice` or `/call` URL). **Phone Call:** tap the "Call" button on the character's profile to start a real-time two-way **VOICE** call; when it ends, the text chat continues in the same thread. **Voice Replies / voice notes:** tap the speaker icon next to a reply to hear the character speak it aloud in their assigned voice.
- **Use-case for THIS article:** The "missing voice / text-only" failure. Many apps are text-only; voice is named in voice-of-customer research as the real-vs-chatbot divider. Continuity carries from text → voice call → back to text without losing context, which ties voice back to the memory thesis.
- **Maps to body section:** **H2 #5 Missing voice / text-only.**
- **How to demonstrate:** Screenshot the "Call" button on the character profile / chat header and the speaker icon on a message bubble. Say "tap the Call button on the character's profile" and "tap the speaker icon next to a reply" — NOT "open Voice Chat" or "open the Phone Call app."
- **HARD constraint:** Real-time **VOICE** only. **NEVER claim two-way video calls** — AI Video is `roadmap`; AI video = generated clips only. Anchor continuity on voice + persistent memory, never video.

### Transparent 3-tier coin pricing (status: live — re-verified 2026-06-28)
- **What it is:** Three coin-metered tiers, modeled openly: **Starter $12.99/mo / 1,500 coins · Standard $27.99/mo / 5,000 coins · Ultimate $49.99/mo / 10,000 coins.** All tiers: unlimited messages, buy-more coins, cancel anytime, 7-day money-back. Media metered by coins on every tier — AI image gen 10 coins each, voice notes 10 coins each, phone calls 50 coins/min (Standard + Ultimate). No tier is unlimited; nothing flagged "coming soon."
- **Use-case for THIS article:** The "paywalls / hidden coin costs" failure. The honest angle is **transparent** coin pricing — naming the three tiers and per-action costs first-hand — NOT positioning as free/flat.
- **Maps to body section:** **H2 #4 Paywalls / hidden coin (token) costs.**
- **How to demonstrate:** Show the three named tiers, coin allowances, and per-action coin costs sourced live. A "failure → why it happens → what fixes it" table is fine; do NOT build a competitor-pricing comparison table asserting unverified competitor numbers.
- **HARD constraint:** **NEVER claim a flat rate, "no tokens," or "no hidden fees."** There is no "$19/mo" tier. We are coin-metered, not unlimited.

### Repetition / looping (no single feature — explain the mechanism; memory layer is the relief)
- **H2 #2 Repetition / looping replies** is an information-gain section nobody on page 1 names. There is no dedicated product to demo here. Explain the mechanism (context-window churn forces a model to fall back on generic, repeated phrasing once earlier context drops), then tie the relief back to the **persistent memory layer** — continuity reduces the blank-slate looping. Keep it explanatory, not a forced feature walkthrough.
