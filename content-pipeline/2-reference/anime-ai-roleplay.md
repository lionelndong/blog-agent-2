## Features to showcase (primary)

### AI Companion Creator — anime style (status: live — re-verified 2026-06-30)
- **What it is:** Build a custom AI character end to end — appearance, personality, backstory,
  scenario, voice, conversation style — then chat one-on-one. The flagship product.
- **Live confirmation this run:** the `/create` flow opens on **Step 1 of 6** with an explicit
  **"Anime"** style choice (alongside "Realistic"). This is the single most load-bearing fact for
  this article: the anime aesthetic isn't a workaround, it's a first-class option in the creator.
- **Use-case for THIS article:** the article's payoff demo is *creating an anime roleplay partner
  from scratch* — pick the Anime style, then define personality + a roleplay scenario (e.g. a
  tsundere classmate, a fantasy companion) so the character has a premise to roleplay inside.
- **How to demonstrate:** walk the reader through the creator step by step — choose Anime at step 1,
  then set appearance, personality, and a starting scenario. Show that the character is yours and
  reusable, not a one-off prompt. Link anchor target: https://pleasur.ai/create

### Roleplay chat with persistent memory (status: live — re-verified 2026-06-30)
- **What it is:** Adult roleplay + fantasy conversations with your created character, with chat
  history saved so you can resume across sessions (the companion stays in character over time).
- **Use-case for THIS article:** "anime ai roleplay" searchers want a character that *stays* in its
  role and remembers the story so far — the #1 immersion-breaker is a companion that forgets. Show
  a multi-turn roleplay where the character recalls earlier plot/preferences.
- **How to demonstrate:** show a short roleplay exchange, then resume it later in the same thread to
  prove memory persists. Frame as the fix to the "AI forgets your character" problem.

### Voice Replies (in-chat) (status: live — re-verified 2026-06-30)
- **What it is:** Per-message playback — tap the speaker icon on a character's reply and it speaks
  aloud in its assigned voice. Billable as "Voice notes (10 coins each)", in all tiers (confirmed
  live on pricing this run). Not a separate mode; one tap on top of text.
- **Use-case for THIS article:** anime fans care about voice/character feel — hearing the anime
  companion *speak* a roleplay line deepens immersion. Pick the voice when creating the companion.
- **How to demonstrate:** "tap the speaker icon next to a reply" (NOT "open Voice Chat"); show the
  speaker icon on the character's message bubble. In-chat capability — no dedicated /voice page.
