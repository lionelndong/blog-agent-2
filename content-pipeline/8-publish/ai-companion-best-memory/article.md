## The Four Types of AI Girlfriend Memory

When an app says it has "memory," it could mean one of four very different things — knowing which one helps you cut through the marketing.

**Context window.** The short-term memory of a single conversation. The model can "see" recent messages in the current chat. Once the session ends, or the chat gets long enough to push old messages out, that context is gone. This is all Character.AI reliably offers, which is why it forgets between sessions.

**Saved fact list.** The app extracts specific facts, like your birthday or your dog's name, and stores them in a database it can pull from later. Nomi's structured notes and memory graph work this way, which is why it's strong on factual recall.

**Pinned or shared notes.** You manually write down key details: a backstory, a relationship history, a preference. The companion always has access to them. It's reliable because you control it, but it only remembers what you bothered to write.

**Persistent long-term understanding.** The app builds an evolving model of you and the relationship over time, blending facts, tone, and history. Replika's emotional-pattern memory leans here, and it's the hardest type to get right.

Most apps mix two or three of these. The AI girlfriend apps that feel like they "really remember you" combine a saved fact list with persistent long-term understanding — they recall both the details and the feel of past conversations.

When you test an app, the fast check is simple. Tell it something specific, come back two days later in a new session, and see if it brings the detail up on its own.
