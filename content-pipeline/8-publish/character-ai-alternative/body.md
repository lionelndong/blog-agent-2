## Safety And Privacy Checklist Before Switching

Before using any Character.AI alternative, check the age rules, data-use language, moderation policy, deletion terms, and emotional-boundary framing.


![Safety and privacy checklist for evaluating Character.AI alternatives](/uploads/character-ai-alternative-safety-privacy-checklist.png)

_Visual asset: v11-safety-privacy-checklist. Original checklist diagram. Target section: Safety And Privacy Checklist Before Switching._


Ask these questions before you share sensitive details:

- **Age rules.** Is the product adult-only, teen-restricted, or general audience?
- **Fictional disclosure.** Does it clearly state AI characters are fictional?
- **Sensitive-data warning.** Does it tell users not to share passwords, addresses, financial details, government IDs, or other sensitive information?
- **Chat-content use.** Can the company use chat content for personalization, moderation, service improvement, analytics, or model training?
- **Deletion and retention.** Can you delete data, and what might still be retained?
- **Content moderation.** What is prohibited, and what happens if content is flagged?
- **Emotional boundaries.** Does the product frame itself as entertainment or companionship, or does it drift into therapy-like claims?

Pleasur.ai's Terms are a useful example of what to inspect. They include adult-only language, entertainment-only framing, fictional companion disclosure, sensitive-information warnings, moderation language, and content-use language ([Pleasur.ai Terms](https://pleasur.ai/legal/terms-of-service)). That does not prove any platform is safe or private. It shows you the kinds of policy claims to read before creating an account.

Category scrutiny is increasing. The FTC opened a 2025 inquiry into consumer-facing AI companion chatbots focused on how companies measure, test, and monitor potential negative impacts on children and teens ([FTC](https://www.ftc.gov/news-events/news/press-releases/2025/09/ftc-launches-inquiry-ai-chatbots-acting-companions)). Common Sense Media also published a 2025 social AI companion assessment recommending that minors under 18 not use social AI companions ([Common Sense Media](https://www.commonsensemedia.org/press-releases/ai-companions-decoded-common-sense-media-recommends-ai-companion-safety-standards)).

This is not legal advice. It is basic due diligence for a category where private-feeling conversations can become personal very quickly.
