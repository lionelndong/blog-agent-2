# Context — why does my ai companion forget

**Validation run (PLE-2983)** — produce through PREVIEW only, DO NOT PUBLISH.

**Audience:** Frustrated users of AI companion / AI girlfriend apps whose chatbot keeps forgetting
names, past conversations, preferences, or story details. They are searching out of annoyance and
want a plain-language explanation plus a fix.

**Search intent:** Informational / troubleshooting. Dominant query shape = "why does X happen +
what can I do about it." Answer-first (BLUF) is mandatory — the first sentence of the opening and of
each H2 must directly answer the question.

**Angle:** Explain the real mechanism in plain language — context windows, token limits, and the
difference between short-term context and true persistent memory — without naming any internal
vendor/tooling. Then give the reader concrete things they can do (summarize, re-anchor key facts,
pick an app with real long-term memory). Information-gain section: a clear, concrete walkthrough of
*what actually happens to a message* as a conversation grows past the context window — most ranking
pages hand-wave this.

**Must-feature (naturally, not shoehorned):** Pleasur.ai's persistent-memory companion as the
"apps built for long-term memory" example — show, don't sell, held to the same critical lens as any
competitor. Hold to brand-config product statuses (no coming-soon claims in evergreen sections).

**Compliance:** Keep 18+ framing where relevant; no "no filter / anything goes" absolutism, no
safety guarantees, no real-person likenesses. Memory/companionship topic is low legal risk but obey
the canonical claim boundaries.
