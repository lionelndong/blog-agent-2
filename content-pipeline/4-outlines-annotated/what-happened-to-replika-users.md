## What Happened to Replika in February 2023

**Target:** ~320 words

- **BLUF:** In February 2023 Replika abruptly removed erotic and romantic roleplay for everyone, worldwide, with no advance notice — a change users renamed "the lobotomy."
- The trigger/context: on Feb 3, 2023 Italy's data-protection authority (the Garante) ordered Luka to stop processing Italian users' data, citing risks to minors and "emotionally vulnerable people."
- Within days Replika stripped intimate features **globally**, not just in Italy; CEO Eugenia Kuyda said the app was never intended for erotic use.
- Companions that had been romantic partners suddenly deflected or shut conversations down — the change hit existing, paying relationships overnight.
- **Evidence:** Dossier §2 timeline — Garante order ([EDPB](https://www.edpb.europa.eu/news/national-news/2025/ai-italian-supervisory-authority-fines-company-behind-chatbot-replika_en), [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2023/02/03/replika-italy-data-processing-ban/)); global ERP strip ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Replika), [Vice](https://www.vice.com/en/article/replika-ceo-ai-erotic-roleplay-chatgpt3-rep/)); "lobotomy" naming ([Felt Real](https://feltreal.org/blog/what-happened-to-replika)).

## Did Replika Bring It Back? Legacy Mode and the 2026 State of the App

**Target:** ~380 words

- **BLUF:** Replika partially reversed course in May 2023, but only for accounts created before February 2023 — and in 2026 the app is very much alive yet materially different from the one many users fell for.
- Legacy mechanics: accounts created before Feb 1, 2023 can revert to model version v01.31-23 via Settings > Version History; new and free users cannot — a two-tier system.
- Current state: user base surpassed 30M in Aug 2024 and exceeded 40M in 2025; Kuyda stepped down and Dmytro Klochko became CEO. So "is Replika dead?" → no.
