## 1. What an AI companion "coin" actually is (and why platforms meter media)

**Target:** ~260 words — **ANSWER-FIRST BLUF section.**

- **BLUF (40–60 words, leads the article body):** An AI companion coin is a prepaid credit that pays for one piece of generated media. On pleasur.ai a coin costs the same on every tier: an AI image is 10 coins, a voice note is 10 coins, a phone call is 50 coins per minute, and text chat is unlimited. Plans run $12.99/mo (1,500 coins), $27.99/mo (5,000), $49.99/mo (10,000).
