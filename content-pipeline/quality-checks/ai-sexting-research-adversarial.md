# Research Adversarial: ai-sexting

Skeptical review of `content-pipeline/1-research/ai-sexting.md` before it feeds the outline.

## Findings

1. **HIGH — Deep-research figures are unsourced placeholders.** The deep pass
   reports "0 citations" yet the appendix carries bracket markers [1]–[15]
   (e.g., the Madigan "110,000 adolescents / 15% sent / 27% received" stat, the
   Deeptrace "96% of deepfakes" stat). The dossier already flags these as
   "leads — verify or cut," which is the correct posture. CRITICAL would be
   passing them through as fact; HIGH because the draft author must be
   disciplined enough to source-or-drop every one. Action: verify-claims must
   independently source or the draft must frame qualitatively.

2. **MEDIUM — "Surprising findings" are mostly category context, not insight.**
   The Replika-2023 migration and "privacy is under-covered" are real but
   table-stakes for anyone in this niche. The genuinely *useful* (not
   surprising) finding is the SERP-shape mismatch: 33,100 vol / KD 4 /
   informational intent served entirely by commercial pages with zero AIO.
   That is the load-bearing insight and it IS captured. Rating: SERP-mismatch =
   actually-useful; the rest = dressed-up-table-stakes. Not fatal.

3. **MEDIUM — Strongest competitor angle is correctly named but not quantified.**
   The strongest competitor pattern is the "no filter / uncensored" product
   page (nastia.ai #1, candy.ai #7). The dossier captures how to beat it (honest
   how-it-works + non-absolute privacy framing, and explicitly NOT copying "no
   filter" absolutism — which is also a compliance requirement). Good. Reddit #2
   is correctly identified as voice-of-customer rather than a ranking rival.

4. **LOW — Data consistency holds.** Spot-checked `ai-sexting-data.json`:
   `search_trend_monthly` (33,100 → 18,100) matches the prose 12-mo range;
   `serp_page_types` (7 product + 2 roundup + 1 forum) matches the top-10
   summary; `intent_cluster_volumes` head 66,200 = the prose "ai sexting +
   sexting ai both 33,100." No contradictions. JSON omits raw KD per cluster —
   acceptable, charts don't need it.

5. **LOW — Brand fit is adequate but thin on owned data.** The dossier leans on
   the 4 product pillars (companion creator, image gen, in-chat voice/call) and
   the "one honest page" angle, but has no Pleasur.AI-specific proprietary data
   to cite (none exists for this term). That's inherent to the keyword, not a
   defect. The `2-reference` stage (parallel) will supply internal-linking
   modules including the `ai-sexting-app` roundup cross-link.

6. **HIGH — Cannibalization guard must be explicit downstream.** The dossier
   correctly says "do NOT re-rank apps; cross-link to `ai-sexting-app`." The
   outline must enforce this — any "best apps" section here would duplicate the
   live roundup's intent. Flagging so the outline stage treats it as a hard
   constraint, not a suggestion.

## What works

The SERP analysis is the strongest part: it correctly diagnoses an
informational head term (33,100, KD 4, no AIO) served only by conversion pages
and app lists, and names the precise four gaps (what-it-is/how-it-works,
honest privacy/safety, ethics, getting-started/etiquette) that no ranking page
covers. That is a genuinely winnable, well-differentiated angle.

## Verdict: **PASS**
