# Outline Adversarial: ai-sexting

Skeptical review of `content-pipeline/3-outlines/ai-sexting.md` before drafting.

## Findings

1. **HIGH — Section 1 ("What it is") and the Introduction risk overlap.** The
   intro thesis already defines AI sexting, and Section 1's BLUF re-defines it.
   MECE risk. Resolution for the draft: the intro should *frame and promise*;
   Section 1 should go deeper (subset-of-companion-category, why people use it,
   text-vs-voice-vs-image today) so it isn't a redefinition. Flag for the
   drafter — not a structural rebuild, but the draft must differentiate them.

2. **MEDIUM — "Vs general chatbots" and "How it works" could bleed together.**
   Both touch persona/memory. Kept distinct as written (how-it-works = the
   generation loop; vs-chatbots = the policy/feature/pricing comparison), but
   the drafter must not re-explain memory in the comparison section — reference
   it, don't repeat it. Acceptable as outlined.

3. **PASS-worthy — Differentiation vs SERP top-5 is strong.** The SERP top-5
   (nastia.ai, reddit, spicychat, theresanaiforthat, dreamle) are product pages
   and a forum. This outline covers what-it-is, how-it-works, privacy, ethics,
   getting-started — none of which the ranking pages explain. The angle wins on
   exactly the gap the research named. Good.

4. **HIGH — Cannibalization guard is correctly designed; draft must hold the
   line.** Section 3's comparison is category-level (assistant vs purpose-built),
   NOT an app leaderboard, and the app intent is routed to the live
   `ai-sexting-app` roundup. This is right. CRITICAL would be a "best apps"
   section here; the outline avoids it. The drafter must not let the
   getting-started or comparison sections drift into ranking named apps.

5. **MEDIUM — Visual count sits at the floor (6 in the 6–11 range).** Four
   types (image, table, external, screenshot) clears the 3-type-diversity bar.
   The external Reddit capture is the riskiest asset (subreddit may be gated /
   selector may miss); if it fails at the visuals stage, the article still has
   5 visuals across 3 types, which remains acceptable. No action needed now;
   noted so the visuals stage doesn't treat a single external miss as fatal.

6. **MEDIUM — Ethics section ("cheating/sin/healthy") bundles three sub-questions.**
   That's intentional (one MECE section for the whole ethics cluster) and the
   research supports merging them. The drafter must give the "sin/haram" angle
   at least a sentence — it's a real query — without turning the section
   preachy. Watch the word budget (~300) against three sub-questions.

7. **LOW — Compliance scaffolding is present and correct.** 18+ framing,
   non-absolute privacy claims, no "no filter" absolutism, SFW/no-people image
   prompts, no real-person likenesses. The image prompts specify exact labels
   and "NO readable text beyond labels" — good guard against gibberish-text
   gate failures.

## What works

The H2 sequence reads as a complete map of the informational intent —
what → how → which kind of tool → privacy → ethics → how to start → conclusion.
Read alone, the headings answer every question the research surfaced, in the
order a curious 18+ reader would ask them. The problem→agitate→solution arc is
intact: the intro names the "every result is a sales page" problem, the body
solves it.

## Verdict: **PASS**
