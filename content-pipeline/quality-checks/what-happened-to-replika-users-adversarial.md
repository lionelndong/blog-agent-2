## SECOND — 5 weakest things relative to what's ranking

1. **No FAQ.** aicompanionpick AND charalt both ship an FAQ; the dossier names "is replika dead / is replika still worth it 2026" as live PAA questions. The draft answers them inside prose ("So, is Replika dead? No.") but never as a scannable, schema-eligible FAQ block. That is the most concrete structural gap versus the page-to-beat, and it forfeits PAA/rich-result real estate the #1 page captures.

2. **Visual count is far below the field.** Every commercial page is image-heavy (aicompanionpick 12, weavai 18, charalt 10). This draft carries 2 generated illustrations + 3 external/screenshot placeholders = ~5 visuals, and two of those are bare Wikipedia/Vice screenshots, not original assets. The dossier says "every commercial page is image-heavy (10–18 images)." The draft is visually thin against literally every ranking competitor.

3. **The alternatives table is thin where the competitor is deep.** aicompanionpick gives 5 *ranked* alternatives with per-app detail across 6 tables. This draft's 7-row table leans on em-dashes: Kindroid, Nomi, Candy.ai, CrushOn, Soulkyn all have "—" in the feature-stability column and one-line "Notable" cells ("Recurs as a top migration destination"). Only the Pleasur.AI row gets real detail — which reads as self-serving and gives a searcher comparing apps less per-alternative substance than the page they're already on.

4. **Unresolved `[link]` placeholders throughout the body.** Roughly a dozen inline `[link]` tokens ("worldwide, no advance notice [link]", "40 million users in 2026 [link]") are never resolved to real URLs — only the timeline table has live links. As shipped, a skeptical reader (and Google) sees a "fully-cited" claim with no citation behind it. The draft's own thesis is "the dates carry the argument" and "you can check against the sources yourself" — broken `[link]`s directly undercut that promise.

5. **The brand pivot lands twice and crowds the migration section.** The stability checklist already plants Pleasur.AI ("Two of those rows... are exactly where Pleasur.AI is built to land"), then the alternatives section re-pitches it with three product links (Creator, Image Generation, plus two internal-blog links) in a single paragraph before the reader gets clean comparison of the *other* six apps. For an ex-Replika audience the dossier flags as wary of being sold to, the second pitch risks the "salesy" read the angle was built to avoid.
