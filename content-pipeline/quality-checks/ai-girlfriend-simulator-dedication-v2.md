## Brutal self-critique (would a skeptical expert editor publish this as-is?)

1. **Defensive over-hedging is the dominant voice flaw.** The verb "verify" and the construction
   "it does not prove / it does not mean" recur in nearly every paragraph. By the third section the
   reader feels lectured, not guided. A great editor would keep the rigor but vary the cadence and
   let a few sentences breathe with confidence.
2. **The intro buries its own best line.** "It is not" is a strong hook, but the second paragraph
   immediately stacks a clunky double source reference ("based on the current search-results
   snapshot captured for this refresh" … and again later "research snapshot captured May 25, 2026").
   The redundancy reads machine-generated — exactly the failure mode the constitution warns against.
3. **No emotional on-ramp.** The piece is all taxonomy from sentence one. For a topic this personal,
   one human beat early (why people search this, what they actually want) would earn E-E-A-T and the
   click. The new hero image now carries some of that, but the prose should meet it.
4. **"Internal next step: `/chat`" register leak.** Slash-route shorthand in body prose reads like an
   internal ticket, not reader copy. Fine as a link; awkward as bare inline tokens.
5. **Repetition of the full verify-list.** The same "age rules, content boundaries, privacy,
   deletion, pricing" list appears verbatim 3–4 times. Consolidate; trust the reader.
