## Why anime AI roleplay bots break character (and what's really going on)

**Target:** ~320 words

- **BLUF:** Most anime roleplay bots break character because they have a short memory window and no durable record of your scenario — once the conversation scrolls past that window, the model literally can't see who your character was supposed to be.
- Plain-English explanation of the context window: the model only "sees" the recent part of the chat; older turns fall off, so the premise, your name, and the relationship reset.
- Why generic character-chat apps feel robotic: shared/one-off personas, no saved profile, no continuity between sessions.
- Name the emotional cost: a beloved character forgetting who they are mid-scene is the single most immersion-breaking thing in the category.
- **Evidence:** research "Questions people ask" + content-gaps (the #1 reader frustration nobody addresses); cycleai FAQ selling "remember our story across sessions" / "Emotional Memory" proves memory is THE concern; one credible external reference on LLM context limits (sourced at verify-claims, never "studies show").
