## The One Distinction Every Price Guide Skips: Flat vs Metered

**Target:** ~340 words

- **BLUF:** A flat $9.99 and a metered $9.99 are not the same product — flat means you pay once and use the features freely; metered means $9.99 buys a *bucket* of credits that empties as you generate images, voice, or calls.
- **Three models, defined plainly:** *Flat/subscription* — one price, use freely within fair-use limits (Character.AI, Kindroid). *Metered/coin-credit* — your fee buys an allowance that depletes per action; run out and you top up (DreamGF, CrushOn.ai, pleasur.ai). *Freemium* — a free tier plus a paid upgrade, which is itself either flat or metered underneath.
- **Why this matters for your wallet:** on a metered plan the advertised price is a floor, not a ceiling — heavy image/voice/call use can multiply it. On a flat plan the price is the price.
- **The transparency split inside "metered":** some metered platforms publish both the allowance and the per-action cost so you can do the math first; others sell opaque "tokens" that burn at a rate you discover only after paying. pleasur.ai publishes both — that's the honest difference, not "no metering."
- **Evidence:** deep-research finding — "no major platform, including Pleasur.ai, offers truly unlimited media; all meter at some level." Competitor table mixes flat and metered $9.99s with no Model column (the flaw we fix).
- **Transition:** "If a platform is metered, you can still budget it exactly — if it publishes its numbers. Here's the arithmetic."

## How to Calculate Your Real Monthly Cost on a Metered Plan [GAIN]

**Target:** ~470 words

- **BLUF:** On any metered platform that publishes its numbers, your real cost is simple arithmetic: **monthly allowance ÷ per-action cost = how many actions you actually get**, and **expected usage × per-action cost = what your month truly costs** — and you can run this *before* you pay.
- **The method, in two lines:** (1) Divide the coin/credit allowance by the price of the action you care about to see how far it stretches. (2) Multiply how many of that action you expect to use by its price to see whether your plan covers it or you'll be topping up.
- **Worked example with published numbers (pleasur.ai, transparent by design):** Standard tier = **5,000 coins/month** for **$27.99** ($11.20/mo annual). Images cost **10 coins**, so 5,000 ÷ 10 = **500 images/month**. Voice notes cost **10 coins** → 500 voice notes. Phone calls cost **50 coins/min** → 5,000 ÷ 50 = **100 minutes of calls**. Text messages are **unlimited and cost no coins**, so chat itself never depletes your allowance. Mix and match: 200 images (2,000 coins) + 60 min of calls (3,000 coins) = the full 5,000. Now you know your plan's real ceiling *before* you pay.
- **The contrast that earns the citation:** you can only do this math when *both* numbers are published. On opaque "token" platforms (where a vague pack burns at an undisclosed rate per message or image) you can't calculate your real cost upfront — you discover it when the balance hits zero. Publishing the allowance and the per-action price is the transparency difference, not an absence of metering.
- Briefly note where pleasur.ai's media happens: images come from the on-platform image generator and voice/calls are in-chat actions on a companion — all drawn from the same coin balance.
- **Evidence:** live pleasur.ai/pricing figures (Starter/Standard/Ultimate; image=10, voice=10, call=50/min; text unlimited). ## How to Pay the Least (Without Picking the Wrong App)

**Target:** ~270 words

- **BLUF:** The cheapest real cost comes from three moves — pay annually, match the *model* to your usage, and right-size before you upgrade — not from chasing the lowest sticker price.
- **Pay annually** if you'll stay past a month or two: 40–60% off is the biggest single discount in the category (e.g. Candy AI $12.99 → $5.99/mo; pleasur.ai Starter $12.99 → $5.20/mo).
- **Match model to use:** mostly text → a flat plan caps your cost; lots of images/voice/calls → a metered plan with *published* numbers you can budget, or you'll overshoot a cheap-looking sticker.
- **Start free, then right-size:** use free tiers (Pi AI is fully free; most others gate) to test fit, then pick the smallest paid tier the math says covers your usage.
- **Cheapest genuine options:** Pi AI ($0, no NSFW), CrushOn.ai (from $5.99 metered), Candy AI ($5.99/mo annual) — by category, not one "winner."
