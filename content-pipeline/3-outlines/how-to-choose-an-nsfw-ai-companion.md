## Criterion 1 — Chat realism and memory

**Target:** ~220 words

- **BLUF:** If a companion can't stay in character and remember a simple detail across the session, no other feature on the list will save it.
- Persistent memory is *continuity* — it recalls what you told it earlier — not a vague "smart AI" claim.
- Chat realism is rhythm and consistency: it doesn't snap back to a generic persona two messages later.
- The 5-minute test: state one specific preference, change topics, then ask a related follow-up and see if it recalls the detail.
- **Evidence:** Consensus topic 2; every editorial + product page leads on memory — research line 73–74.
- **Transition:** A companion that holds character is only as good as how much of it you got to design — customization is next.

## Criterion 6 — Privacy and data controls

**Target:** ~235 words

- **BLUF:** For an adult companion, privacy controls aren't fine print — retention, deletion, training-use, and payment discretion are core product features, and most apps document them badly or not at all.
- The privacy checklist to run: retention period, deletion controls, whether your chats train the model by default, the opt-out path, payment discretion, and account/support access.
- Name the adverse defaults (information gain): Digital Girlfriend AI **retains chat logs for "training/improvement" unless you opt out**, with a **7-day undelete window**; the industry pattern is free/standard tiers training on conversations by default with a buried opt-out, and "history off" still leaving ~30-day retention.
- "Safe" and "anonymous" get asserted everywhere and proven nowhere — no breach history, no audits, no retention detail is itself the red flag.
- The 5-minute test: search the privacy policy for "retention," "training," "deletion," and "opt-out" — if those words aren't there, that's your answer.
