## When it goes wrong: recovering from an awkward moment

**Target:** ~260 words

- **BLUF:** A line that flops isn't a failure — laugh it off, check in, and adjust; the recovery matters more than the slip.
- Common misfires: copying porn scripts (lands fake), going too explicit too fast, a word your partner dislikes.
- The fix: light humor over shame, a quick "too much?" check-in, and dialing back a rung. Mismatched comfort is a conversation, not a verdict.
- What to avoid: degrading language or humiliation without explicit prior consent; pushing after a clear "not into it."
