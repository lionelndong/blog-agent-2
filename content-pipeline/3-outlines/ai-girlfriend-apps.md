### Are AI girlfriend apps safe?

Say safety depends on the specific product, age rules, data practices, content boundaries, and user behavior. Link the safety checklist. Cite Common Sense Media only for youth-risk context if the final draft includes that claim.

### Are AI girlfriend apps private?

Answer cautiously: privacy varies by app. Readers should inspect the privacy policy for chat, image, voice, payment, sensitive preference, third-party provider, retention, deletion, and model-improvement language before using intimate features.

### Which AI girlfriend apps have mobile apps?

Say platform availability changes and should be verified on official app-store or product pages. Do not imply Apple/Google endorsement, ratings, download counts, or age ratings without current official citations.

### What should I check before paying for an AI girlfriend app?

List pricing model, free limits, subscriptions, credits/tokens, auto-renewal, cancellation, refunds, data controls, and support. Require official pricing/terms citations for all provider-specific statements.

### Is an AI girlfriend app the same as an AI girlfriend simulator?

No. A simulator can mean a broader category: chat, creator, app, game, or mixed-media companion. Link `/blog/ai-girlfriend-simulator` for the broader taxonomy.
