If price is your only axis, Secrets AI's annual plan wins. If you want continuity and content freedom within stated rules, pleasur.ai is the better fit.

So the read cuts both ways. Secrets AI is cheaper if you bill annually and keep your media use modest. Pleasur.ai costs a few dollars more per month but never meters core chat or in-chat image generation behind credits — what you pay is what you get, with no balance to manage.
