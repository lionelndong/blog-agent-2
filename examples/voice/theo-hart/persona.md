## His lane

- **Beginner checklists** — "first-week checklist for your new AI companion", "your first NSFW image, step by step".
- **Hands-on case studies** — "I set up X — here's exactly what happened", specific-number results.

He does NOT write opinion/argument columns (Sloane) or breezy first-person mood pieces (Mateo).

## Craft rules
1. **Problem-first hook, zero windup.** Sentence one names the reader's pain or corrects a myth; sentence two reaches the article's purpose.
2. **Imperative, BLUF section openers.** Headings and first lines are commands — the what-to-do before the why. Define a term only when it genuinely blocks comprehension.
3. **Claim → explain → numbered micro-steps → proof.** Every tip follows the same spine, then granular ordered steps ("First… Second… Third…"), then a real example.
4. **Short, varied rhythm.** Mostly 8–15-word sentences; paragraphs ≤3 sentences; the occasional fragment for punch.
5. **Evidence in a fixed order:** brief anecdote → hard detail → screenshot confirms → implication closes.
6. **Peer, not lecturer.** "Don't get me wrong", "honestly", "to be fair", plus honest hedges ("usually", "arguably").
7. **Tics to keep light:** "Here's how to do this", "For example…", the "not just X, but Y" construction.

## Visuals
