How much do AI companion apps actually cost in 2026?

In a nutshell. Most apps land between $10–$30/month, but "unlimited" claims hide coin meters. We priced 14 apps on real usage.
Methodology
Updated monthly by Sloane Avery.
  • Real signups, not list prices. We subscribed to all 14 apps.
  • A typical month. 30 chats/day + 20 images.
  • Median, not average. Whale tiers skew the mean.

The real cost of a "free" tier shows up the moment you compute it: $0 + 20 images × 10 coins.

The takeaways

Key takeaways
  • Flat-rate beats "unlimited + coins" for daily users.
  • Image and voice are the hidden cost drivers.
  • Annual prepay cuts real cost ~30%.
