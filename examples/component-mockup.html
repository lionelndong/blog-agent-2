How much do AI companion apps actually cost in 2026?

In a nutshell. Most AI companion apps land between $10–$30/month, but the "unlimited" claims hide coin meters. We priced 14 apps on real usage.
Methodology
Updated monthly with fresh pricing by Sloane Avery.
  • Real signups, not list prices. We subscribed to all 14 apps and logged the actual billed amount.
  • "Cost" means a typical month. 30 chats/day + 20 images — the median active-user load.
  • We report the median, not the average. A few whale-priced tiers skew the mean.

The takeaways

Key takeaways
  • Flat-rate plans beat "unlimited + coins" for daily users.
  • Image and voice are the hidden cost drivers.
  • Annual prepay cuts the real cost by ~30%.
