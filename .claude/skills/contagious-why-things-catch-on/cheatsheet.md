# Cheatsheet — Contagious (STEPPS)

## The six-STEPPS checklist (Berger's own diagnostic)
Run any product/idea through these. You don't need all six.

| STEPP | One-liner | Ask yourself |
|---|---|---|
| **S**ocial Currency | We share things that make us look good | Does talking about it make people look good? Inner remarkability? Game mechanics? Insider feeling? |
| **T**riggers | Top of mind, tip of tongue | What cues make people think of it? Can you grow the habitat with a *frequent* cue? |
| **E**motion | When we care, we share | Does it generate emotion? Can you kindle the fire (high arousal)? |
| **P**ublic | Built to show, built to grow | Does it advertise itself? Can others see it? Can you make the private public / leave residue? |
| **P**ractical Value | News you can use | Does it help people help others? Can you highlight incredible value and package it? |
| **S**tories | Info travels under idle chatter | What's your Trojan Horse? Is it not just viral but *valuable* (brand integral)? |

## Emotion: share or not? (arousal, not positivity, decides)
| | High arousal → SHARE | Low arousal → don't |
|---|---|---|
| **Positive** | Awe, Excitement, Amusement/Humor | Contentment |
| **Negative** | Anger, Anxiety, Disgust | Sadness |
- **Rule**: make them **mad, not sad**. Add arousal (angrier story, funnier ad) → more sharing.

## Decision rules
- **Need a launch spike?** → lean on interest/remarkability (immediate WOM). **Need months of talk?** → lean on Triggers (ongoing WOM).
- **Choosing a trigger?** → pick the most **frequent** cue, placed **closest** to the desired action. Frequency × strength: a cue tied to many things triggers nothing.
- **"Boring" product?** → it's a design problem; find the inner remarkability or build a Trojan Horse.
- **Tempted to pay for referrals?** → don't; social incentives beat cash and last (paying crowds out free sharing).
- **Want to STOP a behavior?** → never advertise how many peers do it. Make the public private; state the positive norm.
- **Crafting shareable content?** → run the **valuable-virality test**: remove the brand — does the story still work? If yes, the brand is removable and will be dropped. Make it integral.

## Rule of 100 (discount framing)
- Price **< $100** → frame as **% off** (20% off a $25 shirt > "$5 off").
- Price **> $100** → frame as **$ off** ($200 off a $2,000 laptop > "10% off").

## Deal-value levers (prospect theory)
- **Reference point**: set a high "regular price" so the deal looks bigger (infomercials, MSRP tags).
- **Diminishing sensitivity**: same $ saving feels bigger on cheap items — emphasize accordingly.
- **Restrict availability**: limited time, "limit 3 per customer" (+50% sales), invite-only → feels more valuable.
- **Always-on sales** reset expectations → stop feeling like deals.

## Tells & smells
- Buzz but no business? You probably built a Trojan Horse with **nothing inside** (fool in the pool; Evian roller babies).
- Anti-X campaign backfiring? You're broadcasting that **lots of people do X** (Just Say No; "30B songs pirated"; Petrified Forest sign).
- Spike then silence? You had **interest without triggers** (pirate costume, not Ziploc bags).
- "We just need more facts." → No: **focus on feelings**, not features (Three Whys).
- Chasing influencers? → **Message over messenger**; epidemics are forest fires, not big sparks.
- Can't see who uses it? → Engineer **observability** or **behavioral residue**.

## The 7% reality check
~7% of word of mouth is online. Don't over-invest in social media at the expense of offline talk; social media is a *technology*, not a *strategy*.
