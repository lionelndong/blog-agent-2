# Reference prompts — the quality bar (board-supplied, 2026-06-10)

Two proven prompts of the caliber every blog visual prompt must match. Study the structure:
subject declaration → canvas w/ hex → composition map → per-element specs with micro-details →
character/emotion mechanics → style triangulation with negatives → lighting/render spec →
palette block → mood line + quality anchor. Note how NOTHING is left to the model's defaults.

---

## Example 1 — character-driven 3D product render (stylized)

Horizontal desktop landing page hero section for a fictional playful homeware store called "kiln & co." (placeholder). Minimalist editorial layout combined with whimsical character-driven ceramic product renders on a warm pink background. Oversized lowercase wordmark, refined e-commerce structure, joyful-but-premium mood.
Canvas: Full-width horizontal 16:9 desktop composition. Soft warm blush-pink background (#F5D6CC) with subtle paper grain.
Top navigation: Far left: Small minimalist glyph of two overlapping outlined circles in charcoal. Center-left: Three nav links in small medium-weight sans-serif: "Collection · Story · Stockists". Far right: Small circular account icon and shopping bag icon with "(0)".
Massive hero wordmark (dominant, spanning ~95% width, centered): Lowercase "ceramics" in bold modern sans-serif (like Aeonik Bold or Söhne Bold), deep charcoal (#1A1A1A). Tight tracking, clean and confident.
Floating 3D character mug renders (5 playful ceramic mugs with expressive painted faces, arranged diagonally from upper-right to lower-left, overlapping the wordmark): Each mug is a hand-thrown stoneware vessel with a painted illustrated character face on the front — rendered in a folk-art / naive illustration style (not anime, not chibi, more like 1970s Scandinavian children's book illustration meets Eastern European folk ceramics). Characters are charming, slightly irregular, hand-painted with visible brushstroke texture. Each has a different emotion and color palette:
Top-right — The Curious One: A round mug in warm terracotta-orange glaze with a painted face showing raised eyebrows and a small "o"-shaped mouth. Eyes drawn as simple almond shapes looking upward, as if asking a question. Two small tulip-like flower accents painted on the rim in yellow and green. Tilted at 35°.
Upper-middle — The Sleepy One: A taller mug in soft sage-green glaze with a painted face showing closed-eye crescents (suggesting a gentle sleep or contentment), a small peaceful smile. A tiny painted moon symbol on the cheek. Rosy dot-cheeks hand-painted on. Drifting down-left.
Center-middle — The Mischievous One: A larger mug in deep cobalt-blue glaze with a painted face showing one winking eye and a small crooked grin. A tiny painted star floating next to the eye. Hand-drawn eyebrow tilted playfully. Most prominent in the composition, rotated to show the handle clearly.
Lower-middle — The Shy One: A medium mug in dusty-pink glaze with a painted face showing soft looking-down eyes (long lashes visible), a gentle bashful smile, heavy rosy blushes on both cheeks. A small hand-painted heart on the side of the mug. Tilted sideways.
Lower-left — The Confident One: A smaller mug in creamy butter-yellow glaze with a painted face showing bold big eyes looking straight ahead, a wide smile with visible teeth, eyebrows raised in a confident lift. A small painted crown detail above the face. Floating at the lowest position, almost horizontal.
All characters share a consistent illustration style — simple hand-painted linework with a slightly rustic feel, subtle color variations in the paint, visible ceramic texture showing through the glaze. The faces feel like they're painted by the same artist. Cute but not childish — the quality suggests "artisan studio" rather than "novelty store."
Objects float in space with soft cast shadows beneath, as if gently levitating. Photorealistic 3D rendering with soft studio lighting from upper-left, authentic glazed ceramic material behavior, fine brushwork visible on painted details.
Left content column (lower-left, below the "c" of "ceramics"): Top: Price in bold medium-weight sans-serif: "from $42". Middle: Three-line description in warm muted brown-grey: "A small family of mugs, each with their own mood. Because mornings are different, and your cup should understand." Bottom: Pill-shaped outlined button: "Meet the collection →".
Bottom-right: "01 / 08" pagination and "mood collection — fall 2026" in tracked uppercase.
Bottom strip: Left: "free shipping over $120 · 30-day returns" Center: "kiln & co. — homeware atelier since 2022"
Palette: soft blush-pink background (#F5D6CC), deep charcoal typography (#1A1A1A), terracotta-orange, sage-green, cobalt-blue, dusty-pink, butter-yellow (the five mug glazes), warm muted brown-grey body text (#6B5A52). Typography: bold modern sans-serif for wordmark, medium sans for body, small tracked uppercase for meta. Mood: joyful, emotionally playful, folk-art illustrated, artisan-but-polished, character-driven premium homeware. Not anime-kawaii, not chibi — more scandinavian children's book illustration meets eastern european folk pottery. Awwwards / Dribbble quality landing page, 4k.

**Why it works:** five elements, each with its own emotion, glaze color, micro-details and rotation;
style triangulated with explicit negatives; full palette with hexes; material behavior named;
quality anchor closes it.

## Example 2 — cinematic photoreal portrait (commercial photography)

Horizontal desktop landing page hero section for a fictional snowboard eyewear and mountain gear store called "APEX & SUMMIT" (placeholder). Cinematic sports-tech advertising aesthetic with extreme close-up athletic portrait, reflection-based storytelling, bold condensed voice-command headline, premium performance-brand layout. 16:9 desktop composition.
Canvas: Full-width horizontal 16:9 desktop hero. Deep snow-white to soft icy-blue gradient background with subtle motion-blur snow particles drifting across the frame.
Top navigation (thin minimal bar across the top): Far left: Small bold condensed wordmark "APEX & SUMMIT" in crisp white. Center-left: Four navigation links in small clean sans-serif: "Goggles · Helmets · Apparel · Pro Riders". Far right: A small outlined pill button in white: "Find a Store →", and next to it a cart icon with "(0)" indicator.
Main hero image (fills ~75% of the composition, left-weighted): An extreme close-up cinematic portrait of a young professional snowboarder — a woman with her hair pulled back tightly, visible small beads of melted snow on her temples and eyebrows, sun-kissed skin with a subtle athletic glow. She wears oversized performance snowboard goggles with a bold wraparound frame and large mirrored reflective lenses tinted in a vivid amber-gold to deep magenta gradient (no visible brand logo on the frame — clean unbranded design).
Key storytelling element — the reflection in her goggles: Inside the mirrored lens surface, a sharp and detailed reflection of a dynamic snowboard action scene is visible — a snowboarder mid-air executing a jump off a pristine mountain ridge against bright blue sky, powder snow spraying out behind them, mountain peaks receding into the distance. The reflection fills both lenses seamlessly, creating the illusion that she's watching the run unfold in real-time. Small text at the bottom of the lens reads "APEX" in tiny white tracked caps as a subtle technology name.
Her expression is focused and confident, lips slightly parted as if mid-breath, jaw relaxed. Small earpod visible in one ear (generic wireless earbud design, unbranded). A glimpse of a high-collar black thermal jacket with subtle textured ribbing visible at the bottom of frame.
Lighting: cinematic side-light with snow-bounce highlights warming her face, creating dramatic contrast. Shallow depth of field keeps her face and goggles tack-sharp while the edges soften.
Main headline (right side of composition, lower-middle area, massive bold condensed sans-serif, crisp white with subtle outline): Stacked on four lines in bold condensed uppercase sans-serif (like Druk Wide Bold, Knockout Heavyweight, or Tungsten Bold), tight leading: "HEY APEX / CUE MY / 'FIRST DESCENT' / PLAYLIST" The headline feels like a voice command — informal, confident, modern. All caps, left-aligned. The word "APEX" slightly emphasized to suggest the brand as a voice assistant / connected device.
Sub-headline (directly below main headline, medium-weight italic serif, white): "Smart goggles for smarter runs."
Bottom-right corner — co-brand lockup: Two small brand logos side-by-side, divided by a thin vertical line: Left: A generic minimalist mountain-peak glyph (two overlapping triangles) next to small text "APEX" in clean sans. Right: A small abstract circular tech glyph (simple outlined circle with a dot inside — generic audio/tech symbol, NOT resembling any real company logo) next to small text "SONICFIT" (fictional audio tech partner name).
Bottom-left corner — small attribution line in light white italic serif: "Emma Nord · Pro rider in the Glacier collection"
Bottom strip (very thin line across very bottom, tracked uppercase sans-serif in soft grey): "NEW ARRIVAL · GLACIER COLLECTION 2026 · FREE SHIPPING ON ORDERS OVER $150 · LEARN MORE →"
Palette: icy-blue and snow-white gradient background (#D8E5EC to #F0F5F8), warm amber-to-magenta reflective lens (#E89A3A to #C14A6E), natural sun-kissed skin tones, crisp white typography (#FFFFFF), subtle text drop-shadows for legibility, deep charcoal for UI elements. Typography: bold condensed uppercase grotesk sans for main headline (Druk Wide / Knockout / Tungsten Bold), italic serif for sub-headline, clean medium sans for navigation, small tracked uppercase for meta. Mood: high-performance athletic, premium technology-forward, cinematic commercial, aspirational mountain sport, confident Gen-Z voice. Finish: crisp commercial beauty photography, cinematic color grading, sharp lens reflections, subtle snow particles, shallow depth of field. Awwwards / Behance site-of-the-day premium sportswear landing page, 4k.

**Why it works:** one storytelling device (the goggle reflection) carries the whole image; human
detail at the level of "beads of melted snow on her temples" and "lips slightly parted as if
mid-breath"; explicit lighting recipe; unbranded-by-design; palette + typography + finish blocks.
This is the register to hit for Pleasur.AI photoreal companion-scene heroes (tasteful, 18+,
AI-generated persons only).
